Technical Breakthroughs

Addressing standard time-series flaws in watershed hydrology.

1. Same-Day Meteorological Alignment
Prediction cutoff: Uses observed weather on day T and river observations through T-1 to estimate streamflow on day T.
Scope: This is hydrological nowcasting, not a demonstrated multi-day early-warning forecast.
2. Basin-Specific Calibration
Problem: Broad national scaling can suppress local-basin extremes.
Solution: V3.1 trains from scratch with scalers fitted only on the Long Dai training period and strictly lagged streamflow features.
